# Gemini Advanced pricing: what Google AI Pro costs now, which plan includes it, and what you actually get

Searching for **Gemini Advanced pricing** is slightly confusing because Google no longer presents Gemini Advanced as a standalone subscription. The old **Google One AI Premium** plan and its Gemini Advanced access have been folded into the newer Google AI subscription lineup, with **Google AI Pro** serving as the closest current replacement for the paid Gemini Advanced experience. Google introduced AI Pro as a plan that replaces and expands Gemini Advanced, adding tools such as Flow, NotebookLM, higher usage limits, and Google app integrations.

The short version:

- **Free:** $0 per month
- **Google AI Plus:** $4.99 per month
- **Google AI Pro:** $19.99 per month, or $199.99 per year where annual billing is available
- **Google AI Ultra 5x:** $99.99 per month
- **Google AI Ultra 20x:** $199.99 per month

For most people who specifically mean “Gemini Advanced,” **Google AI Pro is the plan to compare first**. Ultra is aimed at users who regularly consume large AI quotas, create media, use advanced coding tools, or want the highest available limits. Paying $100 or $200 per month for occasional chatbot use would be a fairly expensive way to ask for recipe substitutions.

## Is Gemini Advanced still available as a separate plan?

Not under that name in Google’s current consumer subscription structure.

Google originally launched Gemini Advanced through the **Google One AI Premium** plan at $19.99 per month. That plan bundled access to Gemini Advanced with 2 TB of cloud storage and Gemini features in products such as Gmail and Docs.

Google later introduced **Google AI Pro** and **Google AI Ultra**. Google describes AI Pro as the expanded successor to Gemini Advanced, with access to Gemini’s higher usage limits plus additional products including Flow, NotebookLM, Google AI Studio features, and more.

That means older searches for:

- Gemini Advanced price
- Google One AI Premium price
- Gemini Advanced subscription
- Gemini Advanced monthly cost

now generally lead to information about **Google AI Pro**. The product name changed, but the buying question is still familiar: how much does paid Gemini access cost, and which plan is enough?

## Google AI Pro pricing: the closest current answer to “Gemini Advanced pricing”

For most users, **Google AI Pro at $19.99 per month** is the most direct answer to the modern version of the Gemini Advanced pricing question. Google also lists an annual option of **$199.99**, which works out to about $16.67 per month when paid upfront.

The annual price saves roughly $39.89 compared with twelve monthly payments at $19.99. That discount only makes sense if you already know you will use the plan for most of the year. AI subscriptions have a habit of becoming “essential” for three weeks and then turning into a forgotten line item, so annual billing is better for consistent users than for people still deciding whether Gemini fits their workflow.

Google AI Pro currently includes:

- Expanded access to Gemini’s advanced models
- Higher usage limits than the free tier
- Access to Gemini features such as Deep Research, file analysis, image generation, and video-related tools, subject to availability and usage limits
- Google Flow credits
- Expanded NotebookLM access
- Gemini features inside Gmail, Docs, Vids, and other Google products
- 5 TB of Google storage
- Google AI Studio access with expanded limits
- Higher limits for tools such as Jules and Google Antigravity
- YouTube Premium Lite in supported markets
- Google Home Premium Standard in supported markets
- Family sharing for up to five additional people, subject to Google’s plan and feature rules

The important point is that Pro is not simply “a chatbot with fewer limits.” It is a bundle of AI tools, Google storage, and integrations. If you only want to send a few prompts each week, the bundle may be more than you need. If your work already lives in Gmail, Drive, Docs, and Google Photos, the storage and app integrations can make the subscription easier to justify.

## What does Google AI Plus include?

Google AI Plus is the lower-cost paid tier at **$4.99 per month** on the current US subscription page. It is positioned between the free plan and Google AI Pro. The plan provides higher usage than free access, with Google listing **2x higher usage access than the free tier**.

The current listed benefits include:

- 2x higher Gemini usage limits than standard free access
- More access to advanced Gemini features
- 200 monthly Google Flow credits on the subscription page
- Gemini Notebook features with higher access
- Gemini inside Gmail, Vids, and other Google products
- 400 GB of Google storage
- More access to Nano Banana features in Google Search, where available

AI Plus is the most sensible price point for people who have outgrown the free tier but do not need 5 TB of storage or Pro-level quotas. It may also be a better fit for light users who want occasional image or video generation without committing to a $20 monthly subscription.

There is one detail worth checking before subscribing: Google’s plan page can show different storage and feature combinations depending on country, market, account, and current offer. The subscription page currently lists 400 GB for AI Plus, while some Google One comparison views may display additional storage configurations. Always rely on the final checkout screen for the exact storage amount attached to your account.

## What makes Google AI Ultra different?

Google AI Ultra is for users who care more about usage ceilings and advanced creative features than about keeping the monthly bill small.

The current subscription page lists two Ultra levels:

- **Ultra 5x:** $99.99 per month
- **Ultra 20x:** $199.99 per month

Google describes the $99.99 option as providing 5x higher usage limits than AI Pro, while the $199.99 option provides 20x higher usage limits than AI Pro.

Ultra includes the Pro benefits and adds higher access to:

- Gemini’s advanced models
- Deep Think features where available
- Google Flow
- AI Studio
- Google Search AI capabilities
- Jules coding tasks
- Google Antigravity
- Gemini Notebook
- Gemini inside Google apps
- Project Genie in supported markets
- Google Home Premium Advanced
- YouTube Premium individual
- Larger cloud storage allocations

The current comparison page lists **20 TB** for the Ultra 5x tier and **30 TB** for the Ultra 20x tier. It also lists 10,000 monthly Google Flow credits for Ultra 5x and 25,000 for Ultra 20x.

Ultra may make sense for:

- Developers running frequent coding and agent tasks
- Video and image creators using Google’s generation tools heavily
- Teams or households that can make practical use of large shared storage
- Researchers who regularly upload large files and run repeated analysis
- Users who need higher quotas and are willing to pay for them

It does not make much sense simply because “Ultra sounds better.” The extra cost is mostly about access levels, limits, and bundled tools. If you spend $20 per month and rarely hit Pro’s limits, moving to $100 per month will not improve your day-to-day experience in proportion to the price.

## Free Gemini vs. paid Gemini

The free plan is more capable than the old “basic chatbot” description suggests. Google’s current page lists access to Gemini features including image generation and editing, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, Gems, Google Flow access, Gemini Notebook, and 15 GB of storage. The exact model access and limits can vary.

The main difference between free and paid plans is not always whether a feature exists. It is often:

- How much usage you receive
- Which model or model level you can access
- How quickly limits are reached
- How large a file or conversation you can handle
- Whether a feature remains available during periods of high demand
- Which Google services are connected to the subscription

Google’s help documentation says free users receive standard limits, AI Plus users receive 2x higher limits, AI Pro users receive 4x higher limits, and AI Ultra users receive 5x or 20x higher limits than AI Pro depending on the Ultra subscription. Google also warns that usage limits can change and may be affected by testing, capacity, or availability.

That last point matters. A paid Gemini plan is not an unlimited API key. Google can apply caps to prompts, conversations, media generation, and other compute-heavy features. A subscription buys access at a higher level, not an infinite number of requests.

## Which Gemini plan should you choose?

### Choose Free if you use Gemini occasionally

Free is enough for:

- Quick questions
- Basic brainstorming
- Occasional summarization
- Light writing assistance
- Simple image or research tasks
- Users who do not need larger file handling or higher quotas

You can start here and upgrade later. There is little reason to pay before you have identified an actual limitation.

### Choose AI Plus if you want a low-cost upgrade

AI Plus is a reasonable option when:

- The free tier feels restrictive
- You want more usage without paying $20 per month
- You want additional storage
- You use Google’s creative tools occasionally
- You want some Google app integrations
- You are price-sensitive but still want paid access

The tradeoff is that AI Plus has lower limits and fewer benefits than Pro. It is best viewed as an entry-level paid plan, not as the full replacement for the old Gemini Advanced experience.

### Choose AI Pro if you mean “Gemini Advanced”

AI Pro is the strongest default recommendation for users searching specifically for Gemini Advanced pricing.

Choose it if you want:

- A serious paid Gemini experience
- Higher daily usage
- Better support for larger files and longer context
- Gemini in Gmail and Docs
- NotebookLM and Flow benefits
- 5 TB of storage
- More advanced research, coding, and creative features
- The option to share benefits with family members

Google’s help page lists a 1-million-token context window for AI Pro and AI Ultra, compared with 128,000 tokens for AI Plus and 32,000 tokens without an AI plan. That difference is relevant when working with long documents, transcripts, research materials, or large collections of notes.

### Choose Ultra only when you can use the quotas

Ultra is for heavy users. The price jump from $19.99 to $99.99 is substantial, and the 20x option doubles the already high Ultra 5x price.

It is worth considering when you regularly use:

- High-volume media generation
- Large research workflows
- Coding agents
- Google Flow
- Deep Think
- Large file analysis
- Google’s newer experimental tools
- Extensive shared cloud storage

For everyone else, Pro will probably be the more balanced choice.

## Gemini Advanced pricing and Google storage

One reason Google AI Pro can look more competitive than a standalone chatbot subscription is that the price includes cloud storage. Google currently lists 5 TB for AI Pro, while AI Plus includes 400 GB and Ultra plans include larger allocations.

If you already pay separately for Google One storage, compare the total cost rather than looking only at the AI feature list.

For example, a user who needs both:

- A large Google Drive, Gmail, and Photos storage plan
- Regular access to advanced Gemini features

may get more practical value from AI Pro than from paying for storage and an AI tool separately. On the other hand, someone who has minimal storage needs and uses Gemini only a few times per month may prefer Free or AI Plus.

Google also allows family sharing for AI Pro benefits in supported circumstances, with up to five additional people listed on the plan information pages. The exact AI feature availability for family members can depend on Google’s current rules and region.

## Are there Gemini pricing discounts or free trials?

Google’s offers can change by country, account, student status, and subscription history. The current page displays a student offer for a free Pro plan for one year, but eligibility conditions apply.

Google has also run introductory promotions for paid AI plans in the past. Older offers should not be treated as current unless they still appear in the checkout flow. A search result, screenshot, or blog post from a previous promotion is not enough to confirm that a discount remains available.

Before subscribing, check:

1. Whether your account is eligible for a trial or student offer.
2. The price after the introductory period.
3. Whether the subscription renews monthly or annually.
4. Whether storage changes immediately after upgrading.
5. Which Gemini, Flow, NotebookLM, YouTube, or Google app features are available in your country.
6. Whether canceling the plan affects your storage usage or existing Google One account.

This is especially important with annual plans. The lower effective monthly price can be useful, but only if you actually plan to keep the subscription.

## Final verdict on Gemini Advanced pricing

The name **Gemini Advanced** is now mostly a legacy search term. The current paid plan that most closely replaces it is **Google AI Pro**.

Use this quick decision rule:

- Pick **Free** if you are still exploring Gemini.
- Pick **AI Plus at $4.99/month** if you want a cheap step up from free access.
